## 1. Scope completed
- Implemented single unified page component `EmailCaptureView` (`frontend/src/soulmate/components/email/EmailCaptureView.tsx`) adhering to the 390px mobile baseline and matching Figma nodes `102:486` (Prefer Female) and `102:557` (Prefer Male):
  - **Single Component Architecture**: Not split into two independent business pages. A single component handles both visual variants based on `preferredPartnerGender` (Q3).
  - **QUIZ-01 Invariant**: Visual variant (sketch background illustration and gender summary badge) is governed strictly by Q3 (`preferred_partner_gender`), explicitly ignoring Q2 (`user_gender`).
  - **Figma Background Assets**: Exported permanent 2x sketch illustrations directly from Figma (`frontend/public/images/email/sketch-male.png` and `sketch-female.png`).
  - **Interaction Card**: Rounded white card (`rounded-t-[32px]`) featuring purple subtitle ("See your soulmate!"), main heading ("Where should we send it?"), and responsive spacing.
  - **Form Validation & Accessibility**: Email regex validation (`^[^\s@]+@[^\s@]+\.[^\s@]+$`), empty check, and accessible error message linking via `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby="email-error-msg"`.
  - **Submitting State**: Disables email input, terms checkbox, and reveal CTA button while displaying a loading spinner during async submission.
  - **Terms & Privacy Checkbox**: Custom checkbox defaulting to checked (`true`), with interactive toggle and inline links to Terms & Conditions and Privacy Notice.
  - **User Details Summary Footer**: 3 circular badges rendering data from:
    1. Q3: Partner gender (`Female` / `Male` with Venus ♀ / Mars ♂ SVG icons)
    2. Q5: Partner age range (e.g. `30-40` formatted from `age_30_40`)
    3. Q6: Partner ethnicity (e.g. `Latino` formatted from `hispanic_latino`)
- Created page route `GET /soulmate/email` (`frontend/src/app/soulmate/email/page.tsx`) wrapped in `<Suspense>`, reading query params and forwarding to `/soulmate/subscribe`.
- Created automated test suite (`frontend/tests/email-capture.test.tsx`) with 15 tests verifying single component behavior, QUIZ-01 variant selection, Q3/Q5/Q6 badge rendering, validation/error/submitting states, and helper formatters.
